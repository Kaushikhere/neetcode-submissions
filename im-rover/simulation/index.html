T1 Run Simulator

Watch two ways to get from a random start to T1. Your friend's method spins until LEFT + RIGHT = 99, homes to a fixed spot and replays a tuned path. Our method lets the 30 cm back wall set the heading by reversing into it, measures across the box with both side sensors, and finishes with one straight drive. The ultrasonic model behaves like a real HC‑SR04: inside its beam it reads the nearest point of a flat wall, so near “straight” the readings stop changing.

Where the T1 error comes from

Heading reference

Friend: LEFT + RIGHT is flat near straight because each sensor reads the perpendicular distance anywhere inside its ~15° beam. Stopping on the first 99 leaves the robot 10–15° off, and the sign flips whenever a blocked turn reverses direction.

Ours: reversing into the 30 cm back wall until both rear corners touch. Typical error well under 1°.

Turns after the reference

Friend: three or four encoder turns after the heading was set, each worth about ±1 to 1.5°, all carried into the replay.

Ours: none. The second squaring happens after the last turn, so the straight shot starts with the wall's heading.

Position reference

Friend: FRONT = 50 to a side wall, then FRONT = 110 to an up‑field wall that is really an angled 15 cm wall (we give it the benefit of the doubt here and model it as flat).

Ours: only perpendicular readings to long 30 cm walls: back wall by contact, side walls with both sensors.

Your field

Defaults come from the official sketch. Measure T1 on the practice field (centre of the target, from the inner face of the left wall and of the back wall) and enter it here; the same two numbers go into the firmware.
